# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable, technical feedback about code and behavior.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and explained where possible.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through their GitHub profiles or the repository's private reporting channels. Do not use public issues to report personal safety concerns. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.
